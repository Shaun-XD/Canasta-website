## Full description

Turn over the top discard and there’s a decision to make. Can you use it in a meld right now? In Rajasthani Canasta, a successful Top Touch lets you play the top card and take the rest of the pile. The card you leave behind can give your opponents the same chance.

Play one against one, or sit opposite a partner for a four-player match. To play together online, create a room and share its code. Friends can join from Android or a web browser. Fill empty seats with bots, or choose Solo to play against the computer.

Build sets of matching ranks and sequences in the same suit. Time your wild cards, claim your team’s eleven-card Pozzetto reserve, and work toward a seven-card canasta. At the end of each round, the score breakdown shows how melds, bonuses, and cards left in hand affected each team.

Choose a match target and set a turn timer or leave it off. Sort your hand by rank or suit, or drag your cards into the order you like.
